LEGOLAND Florida height requirements

Use this LEGOLAND Florida ride height checker to compare your child’s height with the park’s listed attraction requirements. LEGOLAND Florida is built for kids ages 2–12, so most rides allow shorter guests to ride alongside a supervising companion rather than requiring a strict minimum outright.

Results include rides and play areas with no height requirement, those available now and those that need a little more growing. Many rides list a companion-required threshold as well as an independent-riding height — the checker marks the gap between the two as conditional.

Planning notes

LEGOLAND Florida has the largest lineup on this checker at 38 attractions, and it’s built around younger children more than any other park here: 17 rides have no height requirement, and 16 more use a supervising-companion system rather than a hard cutoff, spread across seven different thresholds from 30 to 48 in. Kid Power Towers, at 30 in, is the lowest bar on the entire site; Mia’s Riding Adventure, at 48 in, is the park’s tallest. Because so many rides list both a companion-required height and a separate independent-riding height, this is one of the more useful park pages for working out exactly when a child can start riding solo rather than with an adult.
