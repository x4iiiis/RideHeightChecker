Data Sources

Where the ride data comes from.

Every attraction name, land and height rule on this site is checked directly against each park operator’s own published guidance — not against third-party guides or forums. The list below names the specific official source checked for each resort and when it was last reviewed. Operators can change requirements without notice, so always follow instructions from attraction staff on the day.

Walt Disney World Resort

Parks covered: Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Disney’s Hollywood Studios, Disney’s Animal Kingdom, Disney’s Typhoon Lagoon, Disney’s Blizzard Beach.

Checked against Disney’s official Height Requirements FAQ and its Rider Switch service page, which covers the “ride now with an adult, ride solo later” rule used on attractions like Tomorrowland Speedway. Last reviewed: August 2026.

Universal Orlando Resort

Parks covered: Universal Studios Florida, Universal Islands of Adventure, Universal Epic Universe, Universal’s Volcano Bay.

Checked against Universal’s official Ride Height Requirements page and its published Rider Safety and Accessibility guide, which sets out the supervising-companion rules used across most of the resort’s family rides. Last reviewed: August 2026.

SeaWorld Orlando

Checked against SeaWorld Orlando’s official rides listing and published accessibility guide. Last reviewed: August 2026.

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

Checked against Busch Gardens Tampa Bay’s official rides listing, including its Sesame Street Safari of Fun kids’-area pages. Last reviewed: August 2026.

Aquatica Orlando

Checked against the official Aquatica Orlando rides & slides pages and its park FAQ. Last reviewed: August 2026.

Adventure Island Tampa Bay

Checked against the official Adventure Island water slides pages, its kid-friendly area pages and park FAQ. Last reviewed: August 2026.

LEGOLAND Florida Resort

Parks covered: LEGOLAND Florida (Theme Park), LEGOLAND Water Park.

Checked against LEGOLAND Florida’s official Attraction Height Restrictions page, which covers both the Theme Park and the Water Park. Peppa Pig Theme Park is not currently included on this site. Last reviewed: August 2026.

The Grove Resort & Water Park

Surfari Water Park is a hotel amenity rather than a standalone gated park. Checked against The Grove Resort’s official Surfari Water Park FAQ. Last reviewed: August 2026.

How corrections work

If a listed requirement doesn’t match what you see at the park, please email ryan@x4iiiis.com with the ride name and park. Corrections are checked against the sources above before the dataset is updated.
