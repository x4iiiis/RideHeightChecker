Florida Ride Height Checker

About

A quicker answer for parents.

Florida Ride Height Checker is a small, independent planning tool built to answer one question quickly: which rides can a child go on at their current height?

Calculations happen entirely in the browser. No account is required and the entered height is not sent to this site’s server.

Data and accuracy

Attraction names and height requirements are checked against official park guidance. Rules can change, attractions can close, and park staff make the final eligibility decision at each ride.
