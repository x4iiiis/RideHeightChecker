LEGOLAND Water Park height requirements

Use this LEGOLAND Water Park ride height checker to compare your child’s height with the water park’s listed slide and attraction requirements. Results include slides with a minimum height, the lazy river and wave pool with no height requirement, and toddler-friendly splash areas.

Note that LEGOLAND Water Park access is a separate add-on to LEGOLAND Florida Theme Park admission — check your ticket type before you pack the swimsuits.

Planning notes

LEGOLAND Water Park is small and straightforward: four of its seven attractions, including the lazy river and wave pool, have no height requirement, and the three slides step up from 40 in (Joker Soaker) to 48 in (Splash Out). Because it’s a separate add-on to LEGOLAND Florida Theme Park admission, it’s worth checking a family’s ticket type alongside a child’s height before planning a visit.
