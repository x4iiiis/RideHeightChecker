Disney’s Hollywood Studios height requirements

Use this Disney’s Hollywood Studios ride height checker to compare your child’s height with the park’s listed attraction requirements. Results include attractions with no minimum height, those available now and those that need a little more growing.

The park includes several different thresholds, so the next-unlock summary is useful when planning a future Walt Disney World trip.

Planning notes

Hollywood Studios has the smallest overall ride count of Disney’s four Florida parks but a wider spread of thresholds relative to its size: only 8 of its 15 attractions are open to any height, and the rest step up across four separate stages at 32, 38, 40 and 48 in. Alien Swirling Saucers, at 32 in, is usually a family’s first coaster-style ride here; Slinky Dog Dash and Rock ’n’ Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets sit at the 38–48 in end and tend to be the attractions that prompt a return trip once a child has grown a few more inches. Because the park has proportionally fewer any-height attractions than Magic Kingdom or EPCOT, it’s worth checking a child’s height against this list before building a Hollywood Studios day around a specific headline ride.
